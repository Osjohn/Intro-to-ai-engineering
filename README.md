# Introduction to AI Engineering

## What is AI Engineering?
AI engineering is building real products and tools using AI models,
such as chatbots, search tools, and assistants. AI engineers connect
models to data, test how well they work, and make them reliable.

## What is an LLM?
A Large Language Model (LLM) is an AI trained on huge amounts of text.
It learns patterns in language and predicts the next word (token) again
and again to produce answers.

## Key ideas
- **Token:** a small piece of text the model reads, like a word or part of one.
- **Prompt:** the instruction or question you give the model.
- **Limits:** LLMs can sound confident and still be wrong, so outputs must be checked.
